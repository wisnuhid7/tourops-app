1. Super Admin (System Owner)
This is the software's oversight role. They control global configurations and have access to all the business's financial metrics.

Responsibilities: Create and delete accounts for other users (including Country Managers and Suppliers), configure payment gateways, define global commissions, view total revenue reports, and modify the site's structure, such as tours.

2. Country Manager (Country Manager)
The regional supervisor. They ensure that everything within a specific country functions correctly.

Responsibilities: Supervise the City Managers in their assigned country, approve new local suppliers, ensure that prices and taxes comply with local laws, and analyze sales in their region.

3. City Manager (City Manager)
The on-the-ground operator. They manage specific destinations (cities or tourist hubs).

Responsibilities: Validate the viability of tours created for their city, resolve local disputes between clients and suppliers, and seek partnerships with new local businesses.

🛠️ Operations and Logistics Level (Inventory Management)
This group is responsible for uploading and maintaining the updated "ingredients" that will later form a tour.

4. Airline Supplier
Responsibilities: Upload seat availability, flight schedules, available routes, baggage policies, and fares for partner charter or commercial flights.

5. Hotel Manager/Supplier
Responsibilities: Manage room inventory (check-in/check-out), seasonal rates (high/low season), cancellation policies, and included services (breakfast, Wi-Fi, etc.).

6. Transport Manager
Responsibilities: Coordinate the vehicle fleet (buses, vans, private transfers), assign drivers, define transfer routes (e.g., Airport ⇄ Hotel), and set prices per segment or per person.

7. Guide Manager (Tour Guide Manager)
Responsibilities: Manage tour guide schedules and availability, including languages ​​spoken, daily/hourly rates, and assign guides to specific tours based on demand.

8. Sights Manager (Attractions/Tickets Manager)
Responsibilities: Manage admission tickets to museums, national parks, monuments, and shows. Control opening hours, maximum hourly capacity (slots), and ticket prices.

9. Supplier (General Supplier)
A versatile role for smaller external companies.

Responsibilities: External companies (e.g., a local surf school or restaurant) upload their specific services, prices, and availability for the system to process.

✍️ Creation and Sales Level (Product Assembly)
This group takes the elements provided by suppliers and transforms them into marketable experiences.

10. Tour Builder (Tour Creator / Product Designer)
The architect of the trip. This is the person who uses the interface you're programming.

Responsibilities: Combine the hotel, transportation, attractions, and guide to create a tour package (itinerary). Define the final retail price, the tour's operating days, and the trip's narrative.

11. Content Editor
The person in charge of visual and written marketing.

Responsibilities: Write compelling descriptions (copywriting), upload high-quality photos and videos, and optimize the keywords (SEO) of the created tours to improve their online sales. They do not handle pricing or inventory.

12. Flight Agent
The specialist in the airline industry.

Responsibilities: Issue tickets, manage date changes requested by clients, resolve issues with missed flights, and connect reservations from external airlines (GDS systems) with the local system.

13. Travel Agent (Travel Agent / B2B)
The seller or distributor (can be internal or an external partner agency).

Functions: Sell tours created on the platform to end customers. They have a special view where they see prices with their discount/commission applied and manage their own clients' bookings.

🗺️ Consumer Level (The End User)
14. Customer (Client / Traveler)
The user who brings the business to life.

Functions: Search for tours, filter by price/city, view their itinerary, make online payments, download their vouchers/tickets, cancel bookings (if applicable), and leave reviews or ratings of the tours they have already taken.
